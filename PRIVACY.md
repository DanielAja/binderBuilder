# Privacy Policy for Binder Builder

**Effective date: July 28, 2026**

Binder Builder is a Pokémon TCG binder simulator and collection tracker for
iOS. This policy explains what data the app does and doesn't handle.

## Short version

Binder Builder does not collect your data. There is no analytics, no ads,
and no tracking of any kind. Everything you enter about your collection
stays on your device, in a local database, unless you explicitly turn on
iCloud backup — in which case it goes to your own private iCloud account,
not to us.

## What the app does not do

- It does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
- It does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- It does not require an account, login, or sign-up.

## Your collection data

The cards you own, your wishlist, conditions and grades, trades, groups,
and binder layouts are all stored in a local database on your device only.
This data is never sent anywhere by the app unless you turn on iCloud
backup (see below).

## Card catalog & images

Card details and card images come from the free
[TCGdex](https://tcgdex.dev) API (`api.tcgdex.net` and
`assets.tcgdex.net`). Most of this data is bundled into the app itself, but
some requests may be made to fetch live prices or fresh images. These
requests carry no personal information beyond standard network metadata
(such as your IP address, which any internet request includes) — the app
does not attach any identifier, collection data, or usage data to them.

## Optional: eBay price lookups

You may optionally enter your own eBay API credentials in Settings to look
up sold-listing prices. If you do, those credentials are stored only in
your device's Keychain and are used to query eBay's API directly from your
device. We never see or receive them.

## Optional: iCloud backup

If you turn on iCloud backup in Settings, your collection data is stored in
your own private iCloud database. Per Apple's CloudKit private-database
model, that data is accessible only to you, in your own iCloud account — we
have no access to it. This feature is off by default.

## Price alerts

Price alerts use local notifications generated entirely on your device.
Nothing is sent to any server to power them.

## Camera

The camera is used only to recognize cards on-device (matching what the
camera sees against the bundled card catalog). Camera frames are processed
on your device and are never uploaded or stored.

## Children's privacy

Binder Builder does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including
children, because it does not collect data at all.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated.
