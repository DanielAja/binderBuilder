Version 1.1

Scanning works properly now. The camera was reading cards upside-down internally, so most scans missed. That's fixed: point, hold, done.

Safer with your collection
• Scanning a page, tapping "In collection" or quick-adding no longer quietly removes extra copies you'd logged. If a tap would remove graded or annotated copies, you're asked first.
• Page scans save the printing that actually exists (no more "normal" Charizards that were only ever holo).
• Turning on iCloud backup can no longer overwrite a newer backup. If there's already one in iCloud, you choose which copy to keep.
• Restoring a backup or importing JSON takes effect straight away, with no relaunch.
• Trades remove the copies you actually traded, not whichever one was listed first.

Alerts and prices
• Price alerts check fresh prices before deciding, and show up even while the app is open.
• Turning off release reminders now actually cancels them.
• Prices can be typed with a comma or a period, whatever your region uses.
• Your own eBay keys now show active-listing prices, as promised in Settings.

The 3D binder
• "Open in 3D" always shows the binder you picked.
• Cards can't get stuck in mid-air or duplicated when you tap during a page turn.
• Motion sensors only run while the binder is on screen, which saves battery.
• Reduce Motion is respected: the foil holds still and the camera cuts instead of swooping.
• VoiceOver can read the binder and turn pages with a swipe up or down.
• Built for iPhone Duo: the binder's spine sits on the fold.

Plus a link to the privacy policy and support page in Settings.
